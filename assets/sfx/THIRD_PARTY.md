# Third-party sound effects

The three files below are adapted from the public HQ MP3 previews served by Freesound, not the
account-only full-resolution downloads. Each sound's creator lists it under CC0 1.0, allowing copying,
adaptation, redistribution and commercial use. Attribution is not a CC0 condition; creator credits
are retained here for traceability. License check: November 15, 2026.

| File | Creator | Source | Adaptation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `record_scratch_cc0.wav` | musicvision31 | https://freesound.org/people/musicvision31/sounds/431774/ | Short record scratch, converted to WAV |
| `rimshot_cc0.wav` | OverlookHotelRecords (formerly rodincoil) | https://freesound.org/people/OverlookHotelRecords/sounds/271208/ | First percussion accent, shortened to 3.2 seconds |
| `airhorn_cc0.wav` | guitarguy1985 | https://freesound.org/people/guitarguy1985/sounds/64476/ | Short horn accent, shortened to 1.5 seconds |

All were stripped of leading silence, faded out, peak limited and converted to 48 kHz stereo PCM.
The precise public preview URLs, transformations and SHA-256 hashes are in `provenance.json` and
`library.json`. Reproduce the import with `python scripts/import_cc0_sfx.py`.
